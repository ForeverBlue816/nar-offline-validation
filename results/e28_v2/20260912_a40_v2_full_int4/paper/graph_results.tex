These random-weight model-forward decode results use the isolated current-stream adapter and a separate matched eager/Graph panel on the same physical A40, with three balanced sessions of fifty formal runs each. All six full-model Graph paths passed growing-cache A-B-A checks. Timings use one preallocated 2176-token page; separate correctness checks use 64-token pages and cross actual page boundaries. For Llama-3.2-3B, Graph decode medians are 15.04, 16.33, and 16.65 ms per step for FP16, Hadamard, and PrismQuant. PrismQuant achieves 0.90x relative to FP16 and has 1.96\% latency difference relative to Hadamard; consistent observed direction in these three sessions. Relative to its own matched eager path, PrismQuant Graph is 2.59x faster. This execution-mode gain is distinct from the within-Graph method comparison. For Llama-3.1-8B, Graph decode medians are 28.83, 23.10, and 23.64 ms per step for FP16, Hadamard, and PrismQuant. PrismQuant achieves 1.22x relative to FP16 and has 2.35\% latency difference relative to Hadamard; consistent observed direction in these three sessions. Relative to its own matched eager path, PrismQuant Graph is 2.11x faster. This execution-mode gain is distinct from the within-Graph method comparison.
