We benchmark random-weight Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B on one NVIDIA A40 with the existing QuaRot integer backend. Core measurements use ten complete warm-up runs and fifty repetitions in each of three balanced sessions. Decode is a sequence of causal model-forward calls, excludes the first eight of128 steps, and is not serving or sampling latency. We report independent process memory and separate wall, CUDA-event and profiler durations. Per-session and pooled statistics are generated from raw JSON; missing or invalid results are not imputed.
