## User-requested hypotheses (verbatim)

H34a  Under SYMMETRIC quantization, Q5a and Q5b are within one seed SD of Q1
      (the target column is irrelevant once the level is flat and localized).
H34b  Under ASYMMETRIC quantization, define the offset-specific share
      s = (PPL_Q5 − PPL_Q1) / (PPL_Had − PPL_Q1) using the worse of Q5a/Q5b.
      Decision rule fixed now: s < 0.25 -> the offset is a minor contributor
      at g=128 and the mechanism section is rewritten around flatness and
      localization; s > 0.50 -> the offset is a major contributor and E29's
      Q2 parity has another cause to be investigated; 0.25–0.50 -> report both
      and word the mechanism as two-part.
H34c  In the anchor groups, the measured range under Q5 exceeds Q1 by more than
      50%, while in the other groups the two are within 5% — i.e. Q5 raises the
      range exactly where predicted, and the PPL consequence of that is what
      H34b measures.
H34d  The offset-specific gain concentrates on massive tokens: the {BOS, massive}
      classes, under 1% of positions, carry more than half of the summed
      offset_spec, and M1 shows a larger offset-specific delta per token than M2.

## Frozen design and necessary operational definitions

- Models: Qwen3-4B-Base and Llama-3.2-3B. E29 activation-only protocol, original bf16 weights/KV, post-RMSNorm qkv and down inputs, actual transpose, fp32 NLL, 64 frozen BOS-prefixed 2048-token test chunks, paired rotation seed indices 0/1/2. g=128, k=max; no calibration, eigenspace or greedy reassignment is learned anew.
- All E29 results are read only. Q1/Hadamard asymmetric and symmetric PPL/chunk rows are copied exactly into E34 with source hashes. E29 saved chunk means, not per-token NLL; token losses are recovered by new E34-only replays. Each replay recomputes the original reduction='mean' cross-entropy and must match the saved E29 fp32 chunk scalar bit for bit. Token-loss reduction='none' is also stored; its separately reduced mean may have normal floating-point summation-order differences and is not substituted for the frozen scalar.
- The complete fixed evaluation grid per model is: Q1_pq_asym, Had_asym, Q1_pq_sym, Had_sym (E29 replay/reuse); Q5a_asym, Q5b_asym, Q5a_sym, Q5b_sym; M1_pq_asym, M1_had_asym, M2_pq_asym, M2_had_asym; and the four matching M1/M2 symmetric controls. The latter are necessary to evaluate H34d's offset-specific contrast, which cannot be identified from asymmetric-only rows. No row is added/dropped based on PPL.
- A bijective permutation cannot move an anchor into an occupied coordinate while keeping every other coordinate fixed. The smallest valid interpretation is a swap of coordinate 0 with the requested target within each group, displacing exactly one residual coordinate and preserving all other residual assignments, G, and the original D vector bit for bit. Both the unavoidable displacement and complete source/target hashes are logged. This is a target-column intervention with that minimal collateral swap, not a claim that the whole residual permutation is identical.
- In the actual Sylvester FWHT, Q5a uses input column 64 (one sign transition: 64 plus then 64 minus), and Q5b uses input column 1 (alternating signs, 127 transitions). These are verified by transforming unit vectors, not inferred from column labels. Chosen normalized columns have unit norm and crest factor 1. Forward and transpose use the modified permutation and unchanged original signs. Anchor residual takes the smaller distance to plus/minus the chosen embedded column, for every selected direction; round-trip uses the same eight Gaussian probes as E29. Both gates are <=1e-6 for every row/seed/layer/site.
- At k=max every PQ group contains an aligned direction. Thus there are zero 'other groups' for Q1/Q5; their range and ratio are N/A, never zero or a fabricated control. H34c's anchor condition is evaluated for both Q5 variants, but its other-group clause is explicitly not identifiable under this design. Hadamard has no aligned anchors and is separately labeled.
- Per-group down ranges are measured both on common, unquantized bf16 forward activations (paired mechanism comparison, independent of upstream quantization) and at the actual row's quantized runtime inputs (end-to-end diagnostic). The primary H34c ratio is the common-input mean range over all down layers/groups/tokens/seeds; layer/group/seed values and actual runtime values remain available. Constant-column asym/sym rows have identical common-input ranges, as do the corresponding nonconstant-column pairs.
- Massive-token selection uses only an unquantized forward on the frozen chunks, before any E34 loss comparison. For each layer, compute the qkv DC share as sum(g * group_mean(R_Q1 x)^2) / sum(x^2), using the frozen seed-0 Q1 rotation on post-RMSNorm qkv inputs; down-site shares are also logged. Select the layer with largest qkv DC share (smallest layer index breaks ties), matching the residual-stream site of the flag definition. At that layer measure the raw pre-layer residual-stream L-infinity norm for every input position. Pick ceil(0.001 * number of non-BOS input positions) globally, with earliest (chunk,position) breaking ties. BOS is flagged separately. Record the selected layer, all layer DC shares, flagged indices, non-BOS massive fraction, combined BOS+massive fraction, and both must be within 0.05%–0.5%. Masks are frozen and shared across all methods/seeds/sites/layers.
- Causal-loss indexing is explicit: input/predictor position p=0 is BOS and its logit predicts the first text token at target position 1. BOS itself has no target NLL in the frozen protocol. Positions p=0..2046 yield the 2047 scored next-token losses, while p=2047 has no scored continuation. The primary {BOS,massive,other} attribution classifies the predictor/input position and labels BOS as 'BOS-associated first-text-token prediction'; it never invents a BOS-target loss. Every exported token row includes both predictor and target positions. A supplemental table classifies actual target positions, with BOS-target count zero and NLL N/A, to expose the indexing distinction.
- Part B gain = Had_asym NLL - PQ_asym NLL; offset_spec = (PQ_sym - PQ_asym) - (Had_sym - Had_asym). Token counts count unique positions, not repeated seeds. Average paired effects across seeds first. Means are pooled class sums / class token count; 90% Student-t intervals use 64 chunk clusters (df=63), with a ratio delta method for varying class counts. Empty classes are N/A. Signed shares use the signed total and may be negative or exceed 100%; they are not silently clipped. A nonpositive total offset_spec cannot support concentration of a positive offset-specific benefit, even if a signed ratio exceeds 0.5; literal ratios remain reported.
- M1 quantizes exactly the fixed BOS+massive input positions at both sites in every layer; M2 quantizes the complement. Unmasked positions bypass the entire forward/QDQ/transpose hook and retain their original bf16 values. The same masks are used for the symmetric matched controls. PPL and PQ-Had deltas use E29's paired 3x64 chunk delta-method 90% CI and also report three-seed intervals. M1+M2 is compared with Q1 using a paired joint influence calculation, not by adding CI widths or assuming additivity.
- H34a uses the frozen E29 Q2 PQ seed SD as the 'one seed SD' reference and checks both Q5 variants separately on each model. H34b selects the larger asymmetric Q5 mean PPL per model (Q5a wins exact ties), divides by the positive frozen asymmetric Had-PQ improvement, and applies the thresholds literally, including boundary values in the two-part interval. These are intervention ratios, not an additive causal decomposition.
- H34d's M1/M2 offset-specific effect is the summed held-out NLL contrast [(PQ_sym-Had_sym)-(PQ_asym-Had_asym)], averaged over seeds and divided by the number of quantized input positions. Report its chunk-cluster CI and the M1-M2 per-quantized-token contrast, in addition to the requested asymmetric PPL table. Support requires a positive all-token offset-specific total, {BOS,massive} share >0.5, combined class fraction <1%, and M1 rate >M2 rate; individual clauses are exposed. NLL attribution locates changed loss, while masked quantization intervenes on whose activations are quantized; autoregressive effects can propagate to later positions.
- No manuscript main text is present in this repository. A separate E34 mechanism reading in report.md will apply the specified minor/two-part/major decision per model, preserve disagreements, and state the permutation/indexing/non-additivity limitations. Existing report sections are preserved.
