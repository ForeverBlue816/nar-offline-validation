(a) Two-dimensional rigid-rotation illustration calibrated to the same layer-27, sequence-118, 512-token window as Figure 1. Ellipse semiaxes are twice the square roots of the two leading eigenvalues (97.5283 and 57.3704) of the centered 8192-channel sample covariance, computed with divisor 511. The left major-axis angle is fixed by the measured leading centered component's angle to the raw group's all-ones direction; the right major axis lies along (1,1). The two panels have identical axes and ellipse dimensions. Bounds start from the 0.5th and 99.5th score percentiles and expand to retain all 512 scores in both views and both complete ellipses. The filled ellipse is a covariance summary, not a point cloud or a confidence interval. Brackets use a separate shared linear group-range scale and show the actual 128-channel raw/PrismQuant ranges (11.656/0.763) from Figure 1e/g; these must not be read as ellipse projection widths. The rigid 2D view illustrates the alignment principle and is not an exact projection of the full 8192-dimensional k=max transform.
