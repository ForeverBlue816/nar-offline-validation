**Unfitted range-law predictions across layers, sites and alignment ranks.**
**a**, Llama-3.2-3B. **b**, Llama-3.1-8B. **c**, Qwen3-4B-Base.
Each point represents one layer/site/configuration, averaged over three paired rotation seeds (1,120, 1,280 and 144 points, respectively). The measured step is the mean unrounded group range divided by 15; the prediction is the paired Hadamard step multiplied by the square root of one minus the saved calibration captured-energy fraction. Horizontal and vertical whiskers are 90% Student-t confidence intervals over the three seeds (df=2); many are smaller than the markers. Both axes are logarithmic, with equal limits and physical scales within each panel; dashed lines mark identity. Blue circles indicate qkv inputs and burgundy triangles indicate down-projection inputs; increasing color intensity encodes increasing absolute selected rank, with k=0 denoting unaligned Hadamard controls. All requested configurations are retained, including coefficient-storage controls with identical rotations that overlap visually. Each measurement uses all 64 frozen 2048-token chunks without quantization. Historical single-seed diagnostics, which use different sampling, are retained separately without invented confidence intervals. Panel annotations give the pre-registered default g=128, k=max down-site median absolute relative error. No coefficient is fitted. The per-layer relative-error table, rather than a pooled correlation, determines the pre-registered accuracy test.
