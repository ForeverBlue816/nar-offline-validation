Raw magnitudes, rotated magnitudes, and quantizer ranges in Llama-3.2-3B layer 27 down-projection inputs. Panels a/b show non-BOS tokens 160–671 from sequence 118 and channels 1254–3301, selected by maximizing the number of channels with median |x| > 1.0 among all 6145 stride-1 windows of width 2048 (21 qualifying channels; earliest start breaks 183 tied maxima). Their independent height/color scales are 0–40 and 0–4. Panels c/d show all 512 × 64 token/group ranges under Hadamard and PrismQuant on a shared 0–10 scale, with no removed or clipped peaks. Summary statistics are stored in fig1_metadata.json. Panels e/f/g show token 416 in raw group 25 and rotated group 0; range brackets are measured from those traces, and g's dashed line is the actual affine INT4 offset fp16(min x). Each trace has complete axes and common y limits. This is a deliberately selected illustrative case rather than an average across layers.
